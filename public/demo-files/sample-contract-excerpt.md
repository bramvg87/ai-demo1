# Supplier Service Agreement (Excerpt)

**This is a fictional sample document for training purposes only.**

**Between:** NovaFoods NV ("the Buyer"), a food manufacturer
**And:** Vallee Ingredients SA ("the Supplier")
**Effective date:** 1 January 2027

---

## 1. Delivery windows and lead times

1.1 The Supplier shall deliver ordered goods to the Buyer's nominated plant within the agreed delivery window stated on each purchase order.

1.2 Standard lead time is ten (10) working days from order confirmation. For orders marked "priority", lead time is five (5) working days.

1.3 The Supplier shall notify the Buyer in writing no later than twenty-four (24) hours after order confirmation if a delivery window cannot be met. Repeated late notification may be treated as a service failure under clause 5.

## 2. Quality, inspection and rejection

2.1 All goods shall conform to the agreed specification sheet and to applicable food-safety regulations, including allergen declaration and traceability to batch level.

2.2 The Buyer may inspect goods on arrival and within five (5) working days thereafter. Goods that fail to conform may be rejected.

2.3 Rejected goods shall be replaced by the Supplier at the Supplier's cost within the original lead time, or credited in full at the Buyer's option.

2.4 The Supplier shall retain certificates of analysis for each batch for a minimum of twenty-four (24) months.

## 3. Pricing and review

3.1 Prices are fixed for the first six (6) months of the term.

3.2 Thereafter, prices may be reviewed once per calendar quarter. Any proposed change requires sixty (60) days written notice and is subject to the Buyer's acceptance.

3.3 Volume rebates apply as set out in Schedule A.

## 4. Liability and indemnity

4.1 Each party shall be liable for direct losses caused by its breach of this Agreement.

4.2 The Supplier's total aggregate liability under this Agreement, however arising, shall not exceed twenty-five percent (25%) of the value of the goods supplied in the three (3) months preceding the relevant claim.

4.3 Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential losses, including loss of production, loss of profit or recall costs, except in cases of gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

## 5. Term and termination

5.1 This Agreement runs for an initial term of twelve (12) months and renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless terminated.

5.2 Either party may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days written notice.

5.3 Either party may terminate immediately for material breach not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice.
