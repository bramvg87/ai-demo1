# Monthly Operations Report - [Plant name] - [Month, Year]

*Reusable template for a food manufacturing plant. Replace the guidance under each heading with the month's content.*

## 1. Safety
*Recordable incidents, near misses, open safety actions, and one headline.*

## 2. Quality and Waste
*Right-first-time rate, complaints or holds, scrap and giveaway versus target, top waste driver.*

## 3. OEE and Downtime
*OEE by line, top three downtime causes with hours lost, and the trend versus last month.*

## 4. Energy
*Energy use per unit produced, total cost, biggest movers, and any quick wins identified.*

## 5. Cost and Productivity
*Labour efficiency, cost per unit, productivity initiatives live this month and their status.*

## 6. Key Risks
*The three risks most likely to hurt next month, each with a likelihood, an impact and a mitigation.*

## 7. Decisions Needed
*What leadership must decide, by when, and who owns the decision.*

---

*Tip: keep each section to a few lines. The report is a signal, not an archive.*
